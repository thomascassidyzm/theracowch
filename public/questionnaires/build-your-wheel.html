Build your wheel

Thirteen spokes of an ordinary life, in an order your own answers set — and then, one at a time, in your own words.

You don't start with a blank page. Your ranked answers build the wheel for you: which spoke you start on, what order the rest come in, and how boldly each one is put. All you do is choose which of three ways of holding it is nearest to true, and then change the words until they're yours.

The order is not a ranking of you. It runs from the spokes where the next chance to choose well comes round soonest — the next meal, the next breath — to the ones that take months. Starting somewhere forgiving is the whole idea.

What gets kept is exactly what you type. Your words, spoke by spoke, on this device. There is no account, no upload and no endpoint to send it to.

This is a starting point, not a verdict. It maps what you'd choose, never what you're worth. Cowch is a wellbeing companion, built on a real therapist's approach — it isn't therapy, and it isn't a replacement for it.
